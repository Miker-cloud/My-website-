# Tarian's — Founder's Vision

> Company: **Tarian's** — plantbased.cloud | AlphaDataCenter.ai
> Incorporated: **2018**

## Original vision (preserved verbatim)

> I want to develop a working demonstration of my international enterprise,
> integrating three core pillars: education, artificial intelligence (AI),
> and plant-based products.
>
> The platform will include a global plant-based marketplace, independent
> vendor storefronts, AI-powered educational experiences, cloud-kitchen
> commerce, and Tarian's LIVE, an interactive shopping experience featuring
> conversational AI characters.
>
> AlphaDataCenter.ai will provide the planned AI, cloud infrastructure,
> identity, data, and verification architecture.
>
> Our initial market strategy prioritizes Japan and Asia, followed by
> international expansion.

## The three pillars

1. **Education** — AI-powered learning about plant-based nutrition, cooking, and sustainability.
2. **Artificial Intelligence** — conversational characters, personalization, and the AlphaDataCenter.ai backbone.
3. **Plant-based products** — a global marketplace, independent vendor storefronts, and cloud-kitchen commerce.

## Market strategy

- **Phase 1:** Japan & Asia (日本語 / English, multi-currency JPY-first).
- **Phase 2:** International expansion.

## Guardrails for this prototype

- Locally runnable, **mock data only**.
- **No deployments, no purchases, no paid cloud resources** without explicit founder approval.
- All external integrations are **proposed and stubbed**, never wired to live paid services.
- Original vision preserved; system designed for future scalability.
